I make AI agents think and act like humans

Human sensing researcher (PhD, CS) and systems engineer (BS, CSE).
I turn what people say and do into AI buyer personas.
As a researcher, I've developed algorithms to estimate attention and mental effort from eye movements, identify EEG markers of autistic traits, compress signals into ODEs, and detect distribution shift.
As an engineer, I've built biosignal libraries and research computing platforms.
I make music in my spare time.

## 🚀 Recent Projects

- **[CS Bridge](https://github.com/cyber-shuttle/cs-bridge)** – Launch VS Code sessions on Slurm compute nodes from your local editor.
- **[CS Jupyter](https://github.com/cyber-shuttle/cs-jupyter)** – Run JupyterLab on a Slurm compute node, from your browser.
- **[Linkspan](https://github.com/cyber-shuttle/linkspan)** – Turn a Slurm job into Jupyter, VS Code, and terminal servers you can reach from your laptop.
- **[Apache Airavata](https://github.com/apache/airavata)** – Run experiments across HPC and cloud. Also the control plane for Cybershuttle sessions.
- **[Airavata Custos](https://github.com/apache/airavata-custos)** – Provision HPC accounts from federated identities with CILogon, COmanage, and OAuth2.
- **[Persona OS](https://marketrix.ai)** – Generate AI buyer personas and chat with them.

## 🔬 More Open Source

- **[Cybershuttle](https://github.com/cyber-shuttle)** – Connect your data and code to HPC, cloud, and local machines to run experiments anywhere.
- **[Slurm REST](https://github.com/yasithdev/slurmrest)** – Dockerized Slurm cluster with REST, plus a Go CLI to manage accounts and limits.
- **[StreamingHub](https://github.com/nirdslab/streaminghub)** – Stream biosignals from devices and datasets, and process them in real time.
